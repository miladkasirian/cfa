# Part 5: Asset Manager Code of Professional Conduct

Source: 2026 curriculum, Learning Module 4 (curriculum L6965 to L7758). Labels: **REQUIRED** = the book says "must" (in the Code provision or its guidance). **RECOMMENDED** = "should," "should consider," "best practice," "ideally," "could," "we recommend."

## 5.1 Learning outcomes (curriculum L6971 to L6974)

1. Explain the purpose of the Asset Manager Code (AMC) and the benefits to a firm that adopts it.
2. Explain the responsibilities in the six General Principles of Conduct.
3. Determine whether a manager's practices are consistent with the AMC.
4. Recommend practices and procedures that prevent violations.

## 5.2 Who it applies to and what it is

- **Firms, not individuals.** The Code and Standards address individual conduct; the AMC applies, globally, to **firms that manage client assets** as separate accounts or pooled funds (including collective investment schemes, mutual funds, funds of funds). These firms are called **"Managers"** (curriculum L6985).
- It extends the Code and Standards "to the firm level," partly at managers' request (L6985).
- **Voluntary** code, meant as a template and guidepost for investors seeking ethical managers (L6980, L6984).
- Firms with an existing code (especially in well-regulated markets) should use the AMC to **evaluate their own code**. It is also aimed at managers with no code, including **hedge fund managers** (L6985). **Unregistered hedge fund managers** are encouraged to adopt it to promote self-regulation (L6987).
- **Second edition** added **risk management** provisions and guidance on **claiming compliance** (L6985).
- Adopted by **senior management, the board, and similar oversight bodies**; covers **all employees**, not just investment staff (L6986).
- Benefits: shows commitment to ethics and investor protection; **protects and enhances the firm's reputation** (L6986).
- Sets **minimum** ethical standards; general and flexible for firms of any size; applies whether or not a manager must register or comply with securities law (L6987).
- Adopting a code is **not enough** by itself; it must be backed by compliance procedures sized to the firm's business, clients, AUM, staff, and regulatory regime (L6988).
- Managers **must** follow all applicable laws; the AMC may need supplementing for local rules (L6989).
- Clients must monitor their assets, but they must be able to count on full and fair disclosure (L6994).

## 5.3 Adopting the Code and claiming compliance (curriculum L6996 to L7008)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| All or nothing (REQUIRED) | Must adhere to **all** principles and provisions; otherwise the firm **cannot** claim compliance (L6998 to L6999) |
| No partial claims (REQUIRED) | Prohibited: "complies with the Asset Manager Code **except for** ..." or "complies with parts A, B, and C" (L6999 to L7000) |
| Existing code | No need to amend it if it is **at least consistent** with the AMC (L6998) |
| Appendix guidance | Firms are **strongly encouraged** to consider it, but compliance **does not require strict adherence** to it (L6998) |
| Compliance statement (REQUIRED, whenever compliance is claimed) | See exact wording below (L7004) |
| Notify CFA Institute (REQUIRED) | Online notification at www.cfainstitute.org/assetcode (L7008) |
| No verification | Notification is for communication and information only; CFA Institute does **not** verify the claim or actual compliance and does no enforcement or quality control (L7008) |

**Exact compliance statement (curriculum L7004):**

> "[Insert name of Firm] claims compliance with the CFA Institute Asset Manager Code of Professional Conduct. This claim has not been verified by CFA Institute."

## 5.4 The six General Principles of Conduct (verbatim, curriculum L7016 to L7028)

Managers must:
1. Act in a professional and ethical manner at all times.
2. Act for the benefit of clients.
3. Act with independence and objectivity.
4. Act with skill, competence, and diligence.
5. Communicate with clients in a timely and accurate manner.
6. Uphold the applicable rules governing capital markets.

## 5.5 The provisions and their guidance

The provision text itself is always REQUIRED ("Managers must"). The Appendix (Recommendations and Guidance) mixes required and recommended points; it is **not exhaustive** and adoption alone is insufficient: "Managers must adopt detailed policies and procedures to effectively implement the Code" (curriculum L7156).

Count of provisions: A = 3, B = 6 (B.5 and B.6 have two parts each), C = 5, D = 7, E = 2, F = 4 (F.4 has 12 items, a to l) (L7030 to L7144).

### A. Loyalty to Clients (curriculum L7032 to L7040; guidance L7160 to L7182)

| Provision (Managers must) | REQUIRED in guidance | RECOMMENDED in guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **A.1** Place client interests before their own | "Client interests are paramount" | Policies so client interests come first in all aspects (selection, transactions, monitoring, custody); avoid conflicts; operational safeguards; pay that aligns manager and client interests (L7164 to L7166) |
| **A.2** Preserve confidentiality of client information within the manager-client relationship | Keep information from **clients or other sources** strictly confidential; take all reasonable measures (L7168 to L7172) | Privacy policy (how data is collected, stored, protected, used); consider a written **anti-money-laundering** policy (L7172 to L7174) |
| | Exception: confidentiality does **not** override a duty (sometimes legal) to **report suspected illegal activity** to authorities (L7174) | |
| **A.3** Refuse any business relationship or gift that could reasonably be expected to affect independence, objectivity, or loyalty | Establish gift and entertainment policies; **refuse** gifts or entertainment of **more than minimal value** from service providers, potential investment targets, or other business partners (L7176 to L7178) | Firm defines "minimal value" and checks local rules; written policy; specific limits (amount per period per vendor); **prohibit cash gifts**; employees document and disclose **any** gift to supervisor, compliance, or senior management (L7178 to L7180) |
| | Multiple business relationships with a client are allowed if conflicts are managed and disclosed (L7182) | |

### B. Investment Process and Actions (curriculum L7042 to L7066; guidance L7184 to L7258)

| Provision (Managers must) | REQUIRED in guidance | RECOMMENDED in guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **B.1** Use reasonable care and prudent judgment | Care and prudence needed to meet client obligations. Prudence = care, skill, diligence of a person in a like capacity familiar with such matters; follow client parameters; balance risk and return; avoid harm (L7192 to L7196) | |
| **B.2** No practices designed to distort prices or inflate volume with intent to mislead | Manipulation = distorting prices or volume **with intent to deceive** (L7200 to L7204) | Examples: transaction-based (end-of-period trading in illiquid stocks to lift performance; building a large position to manipulate an asset or its derivative); information-based (spreading knowingly false rumors; **pressuring sell-side analysts**) (L7204 to L7206) |
| **B.3** Deal fairly and objectively with all clients | No preferential treatment of favored clients to others' detriment (L7208) | Allowed: higher service tiers and products for qualifying clients **if disclosed and made available to all clients**; side letters, sidecars, tag-alongs **if fairly allocated among similarly situated clients for whom suitable** (L7210 to L7212) |
| **B.4** Reasonable and adequate basis for decisions | Must analyze opportunities before acting (L7216) | Due diligence first; basis depends on style (passive vs active); third-party research OK after reasonable and diligent checks of **assumptions, thoroughness, timeliness, objectivity and independence**; understand structure, trading, liquidity, counterparty risk; for complex strategies explain vulnerabilities clearly and **stress test** (L7216 to L7226) |
| **B.5.a** Mandate, strategy, or style: take only actions consistent with stated objectives and constraints | Applies to pooled funds or mandates where client details are unknown; must stay within mandate (no style drift) (L7230 to L7232) | Flexibility for market events is fine if expressly agreed; best practice: disclose such events when they happen or at least in normal reporting (L7234) |
| **B.5.b** Disclose so investors can judge proposed changes in style or strategy | Give adequate information on proposed **material changes well in advance** (L7236) | Give enough time to act; let clients **redeem without undue penalties** after a material change (L7238) |
| **B.6.a** Separate accounts: before advising or acting, evaluate objectives, risk tolerance, horizon, liquidity, financial constraints, unique circumstances (tax, legal, regulatory) | Understand and evaluate the client's situation before acting (L7242) | "Ideally" an IPS for each client: risk tolerance (**ability and willingness**), return, horizon, liquidity, liabilities, tax, legal/regulatory/unique; review the IPS with the client **at least annually** and when circumstances change; IPS states manager's role and review schedule; agree on benchmark(s) and when measurement begins (L7246 to L7254) |
| **B.6.b** Determine that an investment is suitable to the client's financial situation | Evaluate actions in light of each client's circumstances; only appropriate investments (L7256) | "Ideally" judge in context of total assets and liabilities, including outside assets **if the information is provided and included in the IPS** (L7258) |

Note: the IPS is the basis for strategic asset allocation; the client may set the SAA or delegate it to the manager (L7254).

### C. Trading (curriculum L7068 to L7080; guidance L7260 to L7318)

| Provision (Managers must) | REQUIRED in guidance | RECOMMENDED in guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **C.1** Not act, or cause others to act, on MNPI that could affect a publicly traded investment | Adopt compliance procedures such as **information barriers (firewalls)**; keep **pending trades and client or fund holdings** confidential, since they may be MNPI (L7276) | Evaluate company information against the MNPI definition (L7278) |
| | Material = reasonable investor would consider it important and it significantly alters the **total mix**. Nonpublic = until **widely disseminated** (not to a select group). M&A information before disclosure is generally MNPI. **Mosaic theory** is permitted (L7274 to L7280) | |
| **C.2** Give priority to client investments over the manager's own | Do not trade own account before client trades in the same security; no front-running; co-investment only if clients are not disadvantaged (L7284 to L7286) | Monitor and limit personal trading; **prior approval for IPOs and private placements**; restricted list and/or watch list with preclearance; optional ("could"): quarterly trade confirmations and annual holdings statements to compliance (L7288 to L7292) |
| **C.3** Use client commissions only for investment-related products or services that directly assist investment decision making, not firm management | Commissions and benefits are **client property**; test: does it directly assist the investment decision-making process (financial analysis, trading and risk analysis, securities selection, broker selection, asset allocation, suitability analysis) (L7296 to L7300) | Some firms eliminated soft dollars; disclose the policy and any soft or bundled arrangements; consider following the **CFA Institute Soft Dollar Standards** (L7302 to L7304) |
| **C.4** Maximize portfolio value by seeking best execution | Seek best possible overall result given trade size, market, liquidity, security type; choose brokers by commission rates, timeliness, anonymity, fewer incomplete trades, low market impact (L7308) | Directed brokerage: warn the client it may not get best execution and get **written acknowledgment** (L7310) |
| **C.5** Policies for fair and equitable trade allocation | Accounts must not be **routinely traded first**; if a new issue is too small for all, no preferential treatment (L7314, L7318) | Block trades, **pro rata**, **average price**; give all clients for whom suitable a chance at IPOs and secondaries; policy should specifically address IPOs and private placements (L7314 to L7318) |

### D. Risk Management, Compliance, and Support (curriculum L7082 to L7098; guidance L7320 to L7413)

| Provision (Managers must) | REQUIRED in guidance | RECOMMENDED in guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **D.1** Policies and procedures to comply with the AMC and all laws | Detailed firmwide policies put the principles into operation (L7330) | Compliance programs, controls, self-assessment tools sized to the firm (L7332) |
| **D.2** Appoint a compliance officer who administers policies and **investigates complaints** | Officer must be competent, knowledgeable, credible, and empowered; may be an existing employee, a separate hire, or a whole department, depending on size (L7336) | Where possible **independent of investment and operations** staff and **reporting to the CEO or board**; staff acknowledge receipt of the Code, agree to comply, and report violations; training; self-evaluation; review firm and employee trades; act quickly on breaches and work on discipline (L7338 to L7348) |
| **D.3** Ensure portfolio information to clients is accurate and complete and arrange **independent third-party** confirmation or review | Accuracy duty (L7352) | Forms: audit or review (most pooled funds), or custodian account statements and trade confirmations (L7354) |
| **D.4** Maintain records for an appropriate period in an easily accessible format | Keep records that support investment activity, research scope, basis for conclusions, reasons for actions; absent regulation, set a minimum period (L7358 to L7360) | Also keep compliance records and records of violations and actions; hard copy or electronic both fine; **at least seven years** unless local law says otherwise (L7358 to L7360) |
| **D.5** Qualified staff and sufficient human and technological resources | Assets invested, administered, protected by **qualified, experienced** staff; deliver the services claimed; resources to monitor holdings (L7372 to L7375) | Adequate back-office and compliance support and internal controls against fraud (L7366 to L7370) |
| | **Outsourcing allowed, but the manager keeps liability and responsibility** (L7372) | |
| **D.6** Business-continuity plan for disaster recovery or market disruption | Plan scaled to size, nature, complexity (L7381) | **At a minimum, should consider:** (1) backup of account information, preferably off-site; (2) **alternative plans for monitoring, analyzing, and trading** if systems fail; (3) communication with critical vendors and suppliers; (4) employee communication and coverage of critical functions; (5) contacting clients during an extended disruption. Also backup office space and key-person loss; train staff; **test the plan firmwide** at intervals (L7381 to L7397) |
| **D.7** Firmwide risk management process that identifies, measures, and manages risk | Must complement (not compete with) investment management; must be **objective, independent, insulated from portfolio managers** (L7405 to L7409) | Risks include market, credit, liquidity, counterparty, concentration, operational; stress tests, scenario tests, backtests; continuously challenge models and be ready to describe them to clients; small firms should consider **outsourcing** risk management; risk work ultimately depends on judgment (L7407 to L7413) |

### E. Performance and Valuation (curriculum L7102 to L7108; guidance L7415 to L7439)

| Provision (Managers must) | REQUIRED in guidance | RECOMMENDED in guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **E.1** Present performance that is fair, accurate, relevant, timely, complete; do not misrepresent individual portfolio or firm performance | Fair representation including all relevant factors; **hypothetical or backtested results clearly identified**; forward-looking information also fair, accurate, complete (L7427) | Do not claim others' performance or cherry pick periods or investments; give extra portfolio transparency; **GIPS is "a model"**, not an AMC requirement (L7427 to L7429) |
| **E.2** Use fair-market prices; in good faith, fair-value securities with no ready independent quote | Good-faith methods where no third-party quote exists (L7102 to L7108) | Conflict when the PM sets valuations that drive fees: move valuation to an **independent third party**; pooled funds with independent board members: they approve policies and review valuations; without independent directors, use independent valuation experts; use widely accepted methods consistently (L7433 to L7439) |

### F. Disclosures (curriculum L7110 to L7144; guidance L7443 to L7547)

| Provision (Managers must) | REQUIRED in guidance | RECOMMENDED in guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **F.1** Communicate ongoing and timely | Set lines of communication that let clients evaluate their financial status (L7457) | |
| **F.2** Disclosures truthful, accurate, complete, understandable, effective format | No misrepresentation of credentials, services, performance, or investment characteristics, in oral, marketing, electronic, or written form (public or not); plain language (L7461 to L7465) | |
| **F.3** Include material facts about the manager, personnel, investments, process | "Material" here = what reasonable investors would want to know to decide whether to **use or keep using** the manager (L7469) | |
| **F.4.a** Conflicts from relationships with brokers or other entities, other accounts, fee structures, other matters | If not reasonably avoidable, **manage and disclose** (L7475 to L7481) | Avoid if possible. Examples: soft or bundled commissions, referral and placement fees, trailing commissions, sales incentives, directed brokerage, allocation among similar portfolios, staff holding the same securities, co-investing, affiliated brokers (L7482 to L7483) |
| **F.4.b** Regulatory or disciplinary action against the manager or staff | Disclose **significant** findings by **any regulator or other organization** that reflect on integrity, ethics, or competence (L7487) | |
| **F.4.c** Investment process (lock-ups, strategies, risk factors, derivatives, leverage) | Tell clients **and prospects** how decisions are made and implemented (L7491) | Cover overall strategy and specific risk factors (L7491) |
| **F.4.d** Fees and costs, what is included, methods | Plain language; explain methods for **all fixed and contingent fees** and the **transactions that trigger** them; give **prospective clients the average or expected fees** (L7501 to L7507) | At minimum give **gross- and net-of-fees returns** and disclose unusual expenses; retrospectively disclose actual fees, **itemized when requested** (management fee, incentive fee, commissions) (L7499, L7507) |
| **F.4.e** Amount of soft or bundled commissions, goods and services received, client benefit | Commissions belong to the client (L7511) | |
| **F.4.f** Performance on a regular and timely basis | Regular, ongoing reporting **unless the client specifies otherwise**, even with lock-ups (L7517) | **At least quarterly**, and when possible **within 30 days** after quarter end (L7517) |
| **F.4.g** Valuation methods | Meaningful, not boilerplate (L7521) | Disclose **by asset class** (L7521) |
| **F.4.h** Shareholder voting policies | Vote in clients' best interest; adopt and disclose voting policies; tell clients **how to find out how their shares were voted** (L7525 to L7531) | Review new or controversial issues, unusual proposals, votes against management, and monitor delegation of voting (L7527 to L7531) |
| **F.4.i** Trade allocation policies | Disclose **any changes** (L7535) | |
| **F.4.j** Results of review or audit | **If** the manager submits funds or accounts to review or audit, disclose results (L7539) | |
| **F.4.k** Significant personnel or organizational changes | Includes staff turnover and the manager's M&A activity; timely (L7543) | |
| **F.4.l** Risk management processes | Disclose processes and **material changes**; tell clients which risk metrics they will receive at product or portfolio level (L7547) | Regularly disclose specific risk and strategy information per client (L7547) |

## 5.7 Practice-problem lessons (12 questions, curriculum L7551 to L7755)

### Vignette 1: SZR LLC (Q1 to Q6)
Founders hold no CFA charter, yet SZR adopted the AMC (binds the firm regardless).

| Q | Situation | Answer | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ronoldo broadens a mid-cap enhanced-index fund into micro-caps, foreign stocks, convertibles; plans to tell clients in a newsletter three months later | B: violation, client disclosure only | Disclose material style changes **well in advance** and keep managing to the mandate until then; good research and good returns do not cure it (B.5; L7725) |
| 2 | Schmidt sits on Trezeguet's board (stipend, options after 24 months); stock now eligible for the fund; not disclosed | C: client interests and business relationships | A.1 and A.3; the book says the relationship **should be terminated** (L7727) |
| 3 | Ronoldo accepts Sastre's directed brokerage after Sastre acknowledges in writing | A: no violation | Discretionary, non-pension client may direct trades; manager warned and got written acknowledgment (C.4; L7729) |
| 4 | Zane routes Sastre trades first, fund trades after | C: best execution and fair allocation | Routinely trading one account first harms other clients (C.4, C.5; L7731) |
| 5 | Schmidt tells consultants Sastre hired SZR and mentions Sastre's fee discount; declines to sponsor a conference | B: confidentiality only | A.2 violated; declining the sponsorship means no A.3 issue (L7733) |
| 6 | Brother-in-law auto-parts salesman as compliance officer; BCP = taking trade records home nightly | A: neither conforms | D.2 and D.5 (competence, credibility), D.6 (BCP lacks minimum elements) (L7735 to L7737). The solution cites "USD 1.5 billion," a figure not in the vignette; do not rely on it |

## 5.8 AMC vs the Code and Standards

| Point | Code and Standards | Asset Manager Code |
|---|---|---|
| Who is bound | Individual members and candidates | Firms ("Managers"); firm may adopt even with no charterholders (L6985, L7555) |
| How it applies | Through membership and candidacy | Voluntary adoption by senior management and board; covers all employees (L6984, L6986) |
| Firm adoption | Firms are encouraged to adopt the Code and Standards as part of a firm code (Q8, curriculum L6861) | Written specifically for firms |
| Compliance claim | Not a firm claim | Prescribed statement, notification to CFA Institute, no verification or enforcement; no partial claims (L7004, L7008, L6999) |
| Guidance status | Standards text binding; recommended procedures optional | Provisions binding; Appendix guidance not strictly required (L6998) |
| Firm-level operations spelled out | Not as provisions | Compliance officer (D.2), third-party confirmation (D.3), BCP (D.6), firmwide risk management (D.7), 12 disclosure items (F.4) |
| Numbers | V(C): 7 years recommended absent rules (L4491); IPS at least annually (L3126) | 7 years (L7360); IPS review annually (L7250); quarterly reporting within 30 days (L7517) |

Individual-level analogues: A.1 and A.2 resemble III(A) and III(E); B.2 is II(B); B.3 is III(B); B.4 is V(A); B.5 and B.6 are III(C); C.1 is II(A); C.2 is VI(B); E.1 is III(D); F.4.a is VI(A). This mapping is a study aid, not stated in the reading.
